# Your first week

## Day 1

Get access to the vault, PagerDuty (observer role) and the monorepo. Run the web client locally with `make dev`.

## Day 2 and 3

Read ADR 0001 to 0009 in order; they explain why the datastore is PostgreSQL, why deploys are blue-green and why the workers live in their own repository.

## Day 4

Shadow the on-call handover on Monday at 10:00 UTC and read the deploy runbook.

## Day 5

Ship a small change through `deployctl release` with your buddy watching.
