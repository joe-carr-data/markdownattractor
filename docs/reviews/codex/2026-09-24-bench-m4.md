## 1. Findings (Codex, condensed; file:line against `b7dae80`)

| # | Sev | Finding | Fix |

## 2. Answers (condensed)

- **(a)** Scorer fair and deterministic on archived lists; drivers must hand back repository-relative paths (they do); the qmd 20→40 and graphify 2,000→8,000 escalations both serve the ten-page rule and are not equal-work budgets; determinism of qmd/graphify across caches, inference implementations and restarts is not established by the wrappers — the runbook's rerun paragraph is worded accordingly.
- **(b)** T1 routing is right (`results_dir`, test split, `probe_ids`); the archive sufficed for metric regeneration and now also for request/timing replay (F7).
- **(c)** `--check` is necessary, not sufficient: it binds `config.toml` bytes, the version (the preflight binds the executable's hash), the graph hashes through records; qmd model files, installed competitor code and (until F5) the BM25 tables were not bound. Re-writing the freeze after a driver fix with identical inputs is acceptable when the other arms' rows were produced by unchanged drivers; both freezes are kept in git history and each arm's manifest names its commit.
- **(d)** Intervals and the paired target are correct; row intervals quantify question sampling, not model or build randomness; "met on the point estimate" is honest and an interval containing zero does not establish equivalence.
- **(e)** With two valid scores "mean ≥ 1" implements §2.3; a single surviving score must not stand in (F8).
- **(f)** Disclose: reuse of the M2 artifacts, hashes, graph build failures, the freeze chronology, retrieval caps, the sourceless-node mapping, missing queries, panel completeness and agreement; keep first-pass MCP latency separate from the cost of the scored list; "cold" means a new process, not cleared caches; concurrent chains mean contention. For Tailwind, write plainly "BM25-over-files 0.766, hybrid 0.702 on the same 47 test questions", publish the paired wins/losses and interval under "where we lose", and do not reopen tuning on a test result.
