## Findings and what changed in the plan

| # | Sev | Finding (condensed) | Applied |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | High | Single-section paraphrase questions, Claude grading Claude against Claude-written references; three shared terms and two "answerable" votes prove neither difficulty nor independence. | The panel verifies four things per candidate (a corpus-specific information need; standalone, unambiguous wording; not leading; the supporting facts independently identified by each member and matching the writer's). The page discloses that the questions are synthetic and that the writer and the answering model share a model family. |
| 2 | High | An 80-word generated reference drops qualifications; gate (b) would measure agreement with a compressed answer. | The section's exact text is the authoritative evidence; the writer lists the question's **required facts**, each with its supporting span in the section; both panel members validate the facts and spans independently; grading scores against the validated facts with the section text supplied as evidence. Gate (b) keeps 4.0, labelled as scored against a synthetic, panel-validated reference. |
| 3 | Med | Section-first seeded order favours section-rich documents; "accept until 25" selects panel-friendly material; generated indexes and hash listings yield trivial questions. | Seeded order over documents first, then sections within the document; exclusions frozen before the draw (`docs/index.md`, `docs/handoffs/`, hash and provenance listings, sections that are tables or lists only); every rejection with its reason and the denominators committed; a corpus that exhausts its candidates publishes with the count reached. The result is restricted explicitly to single-section lookup questions. |
| 4 | High | The plan skipped the published prerequisite: Astra calibration before any T2 reuse; `panel.sh regrade` reads `.reference` from the manifest's question file, which for the private corpus is hashed. | A T6 **development pilot** on disjoint seeded questions (10 per public corpus) before the freeze: answered once per arm, graded by Sonnet and both members; agreement published and the resolution rule declared from it before the freeze. Both graders read the same hash-verified materialised question file (`T2_QUESTIONS_FULL`). T2's test answers are never re-graded. |
| 5 | High | Grounding read DocsQA's corpus list, probes read its split and questions, the freeze read its dataset files; "no `--open-holdout`" proves nothing about those reads. | T6 has explicit inputs for everything (question file, page inventory, probe questions, card audit, freeze); `lib.sh` takes the dataset location from `DOCSQA_DATA`, and `t6.sh` sets it to an unreadable path so any DocsQA read dies; a smoke run under `make check` proves T6's paths do not read DocsQA. T1/T2 artifacts are preserved by hash without invoking their scoring paths. |
| 6 | High | The resolver's exact-path branches accept any existing file (traversal included); only suffix matching used the universe; a larger universe adds ambiguous `index.md`/`README.md`; the citation regex mishandles spaces. | A new resolver for T6: canonical containment in the copy **and** membership in the frozen page inventory for every resolution; ambiguous suffixes rejected; tests for duplicate basenames, traversal, symlinks, unusual names and oversized pages; stores and arm artifacts excluded from the inventory. |
| 7 | High | "Factual claims about the product" is underspecified for fictional operational notes and private research; broad markdown inclusion admits instructions and generated listings as evidence. | The T6 reading is preregistered in the rubric: factual claims about the corpus's subject matter (procedures, decisions, values, dates, names, commands, statements that the notes say something); instructions inside source pages are data; the page publishes indexed-page versus eligible-question coverage; the section is labelled a T6 rubric adaptation of T2, not "byte for byte". |
| 8 | High | Private leakage through the manifest: `project`, `question_ids`, `questions_file`, `questions_full`, every `launches.*` field (cwd, flags, mcp command/args/env, project_settings, system_prompt). | Every committed private artifact is produced by an **allowlisted export schema** (opaque ids, fixed arm names, hex digests, numbers, fixed enums); full manifests, launch records and traces stay under `$RUN`. |
| 9 | High | Hashing `answer` leaves private text in `q`, `stderr`, `grade.out.note`, `grounding.*.note/unsupported_claims`, `batches[].pages`, `unresolved_citations`, `oversized_pages`, `cited_pages[].*`, ids, trace names. | The grade exports are built from approved scalar fields only (digests for texts, counts for evidence lists); nothing is sanitised by deleting known keys. |
| 10 | High | More private surfaces: candidate texts and rationales, regrade samples, member notes, card samples and member values, card summaries' page/heading, analyses' paths and ids. | Same export rule for every file: opaque ids, digests, numeric scores and aggregates, fixed enums; a recursive schema check rejects unknown fields; analyses are re-rooted (paths replaced). |
| 11 | High | Committed private probe traces leak questions, answers, tool arguments and results; preflight reports leak through `sample`, `attempt_dir`, `checks[].detail`, probe `trace/launch/question_id`. | Every private phase runs outside the repository; only a sanitised report (check names and statuses, activation counts, trace digests) is exported; full traces and error logs stay private. |
| 12 | High | The leak scan covered too little (`evals/results/own/` only; root and paths only); a public `make check` cannot rebuild a private denylist. | Before commit, `t6.sh leak-scan` checks the **whole proposed change** (`git diff` of the branch plus untracked files, filenames and decoded JSON) against a private denylist kept under `$RUN` (root-path variants, every relative path, every heading, the repository name, source excerpts) and reports counts only; public CI enforces the export schema and fixed allowed strings (common-heading collisions handled by the schema, not the denylist). |
| 13 | Med | A whole-corpus fingerprint lets a guessed snapshot be confirmed; per-heading digests are dictionary-testable; one freeze needing private inputs blocks public verification. | The private commitment is `sha256(salt ‖ serialisation)` with the salt under `$RUN`, the serialisation specified exactly; no per-path or per-heading digests leave the machine; **two freezes**: `evals/results/own/T6/FROZEN.md` (public corpora, publicly checkable) and a private freeze under `$RUN` whose hash alone is recorded; the private table is exempt from independent reproduction and says so. |
| 14 | High | The freeze omitted: binary/source binding, the analysis implementation, embedding revision and files, effective configs and ignore rules, resolved model ids, prompts and schemas, arm versions/skills/hooks, card provenance, page inventory, hardware/OS. | All listed explicitly in the T6 freeze block; preflight, run and resume bound to the freeze, the binary's hash, the corpus inventory, the stores, the served graphs, the qmd indexes and the fixed run parameters (T5's pattern). |
| 15 | High | Naming the docs commit identifies the snapshot but verifies nothing about the copy; `.mda-pinned` proves nothing; equal section counts miss different content or the pre-existing golden cards. | The preflight compares every arm's copy (inventory and per-file content hash) against `git archive` of the frozen commit; inherited `.markdownattractor` stores or extras fail; the T6 card export is selected and hashed explicitly (never `evals/golden/cards.json`); section identities, attached cards and embedding completeness are verified, not counts. |
| 16 | High | "Every attempt's consumption summed" is false for `t2.sh`: the row takes the final attempt's metrics, only `cost_usd_all_attempts` sums; malformed traces can leave missing rows; `wait` ignores worker status. | The plan states T2's actual accounting; every attempt's trace is kept (`.attemptN`); `t6.sh grade` refuses an incomplete manifest (`status.missing == 0`) and a row file with no parsable result is written as an error row before grading. |
| 17 | Med | "The T2 freeze still checks" is untrue on today's tree; editing `t2.sh`/`lib.sh`/`freeze.sh` changes nothing about that. | Reworded: historical freezes are verified at their frozen source revisions and by their artifact hashes; T6 has its own freeze and preflight before any counted row; the runbook is updated in every harness, freeze or table change. |
| 18 | Med | A table-only block test passes after losing captions, disclosures or archived blocks. | `page-blocks.sh` inventories headings, captions ("Generated by …" / "Rendered …" paragraphs), every table and every "What travels" body across the page and the archive; the T5 `tables + caption` hash is re-checked at publication from the archived artifacts; moved links are checked by the docs link test. |
| 19 | Med | Failed-gate medians beside README headline claims violate §7's "limited to gates passed". | Row wording as recommended: T2 "No comparator pair passed all three gates; no savings claimed." · T5 "No pair met the correctness-and-effort criterion." · T6 passing public pairs only, with denominators; private supporting-only · T4 "Prisma, 20 edits, one machine: raw-searchable p50 1.7 s; card-ready p50 8.4 s" (the quantile convention disclosed) · T1 "Hybrid point estimates met or exceeded qmd full on all projects; all paired intervals included zero." |
| 20 | Med | Different corpora with differently generated workloads cannot identify a causal break-even; "the model cannot know them" is unsupported. | The reading is descriptive (corpus-size observations), the questions labelled a T2 adaptation, prior knowledge described per corpus without asserting its absence, no crossover claimed. |
| 21 | Med | "Every call through the Claude Code login" conflicts with Astra through Codex. | Reworded everywhere: Claude calls through the owner's Claude Code login, Astra through the owner's Codex login, no provider keys; env sanitisation on every new subprocess. |
| 22 | Med | The estimates (1–2 h indexing, candidate acceptance, grading time, graphify completion) are unsupported; M8's inline build took 26.6 h. | Estimates come from the development pilot (daemon concurrency, acceptance rate, grounding batches, retries measured) and are written into the plan before the freeze; failed-build costs are retained; the owed independent reruns and unmet strategy exits stay open. |
