| 4 | Medium | Link resolution could pick the wrong indexed document: `//host/guide` as a local path, `guide/` as `guide.md` when `guide/index.md` exists, `/` and a `..` landing on the root missing `index.md`, `manual.pdf` matching `manual.pdf.md`. | **Fixed.** A target with an authority is not local; a folder target tries the folder's page first; the root tries its index page; a target with an extension is only ever itself.
